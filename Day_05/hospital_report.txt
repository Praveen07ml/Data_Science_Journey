HOSPITAL APPOINTMENT ANALYSIS REPORT

Patient No Show Analysis:
The overall no show rate for the hospital in 2024 was 22 percent.
Cardiology department had the highest no show rate at 28 percent.
Orthopedics had a no show rate of 21 percent.
Pediatrics had the lowest no show rate at 12 percent.
Patients aged 18 to 30 had the highest no show tendency at 35 percent.
Senior patients above 60 had the lowest no show rate at 8 percent.
Rural patients showed 40 percent higher no show rates than urban patients.
SMS reminders sent 24 hours before reduced no shows by 25 percent.
Monday appointments had highest no show rate at 30 percent.
Friday afternoon appointments had lowest no show rate at 10 percent.

ER Operations Analysis:
Average ER wait time was 48 minutes overall.
Peak hours between 6 PM and 10 PM showed wait times of 75 minutes.
Weekend evenings had highest patient volume with 150 cases per day.
Staff shortage during night shifts increased wait time by 35 minutes.
Triage level 1 patients were seen within 5 minutes on average.
Triage level 3 patients waited an average of 52 minutes.

Billing and Revenue:
Total hospital revenue in Q4 was 2.3 crore rupees.
32 percent of invoices remained unpaid after 30 days.
Cardiology generated highest revenue at 45 lakh rupees per month.
Billing disputes were highest in surgery department at 18 percent.
Insurance claim rejection rate was 12 percent overall.
Digital payment adoption increased by 40 percent in 2024.

Department Performance:
Neurology had average patient satisfaction score of 4.2 out of 5.
Emergency department handled 5400 cases in Q4.
ICU bed occupancy reached 92 percent during winter months.
Orthopedics had longest average treatment time at 45 minutes per patient.
Pediatrics had highest patient satisfaction at 4.7 out of 5.
